Warfarin Dose Prediction using Machine Learning: Clinical Validation Study

Principal Investigator: LAURA SHEHAJ
Date: April 23, 2026
Status: Research Use Only. Not for clinical use without external validation.

Abstract

Background: Warfarin has a narrow therapeutic index and high inter-patient variability. The IWPC clinical algorithm remains standard of care but achieves only 45.2% of patients within 20% of stable therapeutic dose.

Objective: To develop and validate a machine learning model for individualized warfarin dose prediction and compare performance to the IWPC clinical algorithm, with emphasis on high-risk subgroups.
Results: XGBoost MAE 9.55 mg/week, 23.2% reduction vs IWPC. Within-20% accuracy 42.0% vs 11.0%.

Key Pharmacology Validated:
VKORC1 AA reduces dose 6.07 mg/week vs GG increases 6.67 mg/week
CYP2C9 3/3 reduces dose 14.43 mg/week — largest single effect
Amiodarone reduces dose 3.82 mg/week

Critical Failure Modes:
Poor Metabolizers: CYP2C9 3/3 MAE 38.2 mg/week. Model unusable.
Dose Extremes: 70% of High-dose and 53% of Low-dose patients misclassified into Medium bin.

Clinical Recommendation: Use as decision support only for Medium-dose patients 21-49 mg/week where recall = 89%. For predicted Low <21 or High >49, or any patient with , require hematology/pharmacy review + INR day 3. Model does not replace clinical judgment or INR titration.


Deployment: Use XGBoost model as clinical decision support, not replacement for clinician judgment.
Genotyping: Prioritize VKORC1/CYP2C9 testing for patients with weight >100kg, age >80, or on amiodarone.
Safety: Flag predictions with  or  for INR check within 72h.
Monitoring: Implement model monitoring for data drift. Retrain if population BMI or genotype prevalence shifts >20%.

Limitations

External Validation: Single dataset. Requires validation on non-IWPC cohorts.
Missing Data: 29.5% of VKORC1 genotypes were . 27.2% missing amiodarone status. Model assumes absence.
Rare Variants: CYP2C95, 6, *11 not captured. May underdose carriers.
Regulatory: This is research software. Not FDA-cleared for clinical use.
